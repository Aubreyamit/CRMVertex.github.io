Northwind

Lead product designer · 2024

A ground-up redesign of a regional bank's mobile app. The brief was simple to state and hard to do: make moving money feel calm. I rebuilt the transfer flow around a single running list of "who you pay," cutting the average transfer from nine taps to three and reducing support tickets about failed transfers by 31% in the first quarter.
